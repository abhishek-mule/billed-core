# PILOT_NOTES.md

Merchant feedback only. Not bugs. Not features. Just observations — what the
merchant did, hesitated on, skipped, or asked for, in their own words where possible.

Rules:
- Record observations, not interpretations. "Clicked Recover immediately" not
  "Merchant didn't understand the queue."
- Never turn an observation into a fix here. Fixes live in the backlog.
- After ~5 merchants, read this file top-to-bottom and let *patterns* drive the next
  sprint — not ideas.

Format:

```
Merchant N — <name/vertical>
<date>

- <observation>
- <observation>
```
