# BillZo Logo Design Specification

## Brand Identity
- **Product**: AI Recovery OS for Indian MSMEs
- **Core**: Debt recovery, invoices, UPI payments, WhatsApp communications
- **Audience**: Indian SMEs (trust, clarity, speed)
- **Vibe**: Modern, tech-forward, approachable, distinctly Indian fintech

## Design Directions to Explore

### Direction 1: Arrow + Shield (Recovery + Protection)
- Upward arrow forming a shield shape
- Symbolizes: money returning + business protection
- Colors: Trust Blue (#1E3A8A) + Success Green (#059669)

### Direction 2: Rupee + Circuit (AI Finance)
- ₹ symbol with neural network nodes/circuit traces
- Symbolizes: AI-powered financial recovery
- Colors: Deep Indigo (#312E81) + Warm Amber (#F59E0B)

### Direction 3: Chat Bubble + Check (WhatsApp Core)
- WhatsApp-style chat bubble with checkmark
- Symbolizes: communication channel + resolution
- Colors: Teal (#0D9488) + Coral (#F97316)

### Direction 4: Abstract "B" Flow (Modern Minimal)
- Letter B formed by flowing lines
- Symbolizes: money flow, automation, forward motion
- Colors: Gradient Teal to Blue → Blue

### Direction 5: Invoice → Coin (Literal Journey)
- Document transforming into coin/rupee
- Symbolizes: invoice to recovery journey
- Colors: Professional Blue + Green

## Technical Requirements
- **Formats**: SVG (primary), PNG (fallback)
- **Variants**: Full lockup (icon + wordmark), icon only, wordmark only
- **Sizes**: 32px (favicon), 192px (PWA), 512px (app store), SVG scalable
- **Backgrounds**: Light, dark, transparent
- **Typography**: Modern, geometric sans-serif (Inter, Space Grotesk, or similar)

## Color Palette Options

### Option A: Trust Fintech
- Primary: #1E3A8A (Deep Blue)
- Secondary: #059669 (Emerald Green)
- Accent: #F59E0B (Amber)

### Option B: Modern Indian
- Primary: #312E81 (Indigo)
- Secondary: #0D9488 (Teal)
- Accent: #F97316 (Coral)

### Option C: Fresh Energy
- Primary: #0891B2 (Cyan)
- Secondary: #6366F1 (Indigo)
- Accent: #22C55E (Green)

## Deliverables
1. 5 distinct logo concepts (SVG)
2. Color palette specs with hex/rgb
3. Typography specs
4. Usage guidelines (clear space, minimum size, don'ts)
5. Figma-compatible export
